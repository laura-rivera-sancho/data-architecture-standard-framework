# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected behavior

- communicate constructively and respectfully
- welcome questions and different levels of experience
- critique ideas and implementations rather than people
- protect privacy and never share confidential or personal information
- acknowledge mistakes and help resolve disagreements professionally

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on the GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with appropriate confidentiality. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
